How estimates work
Each leg uses its own market's received volume divided by paid volume. Received quantities round down to whole items. Profit is valued in divines using the same hour's exchange rate. Hourly volume describes past trading activity, not available stock. Historical extremes may have occurred at different times. Gold is shown separately from currency profit.
