    How estimates work
    Gift prices are the cheapest sampled instant-buyout listings at exactly item level 65 or 80, queried separately in each currency. Splinter and Breachstone prices use each currency pair’s hourly traded volumes. Currency values use the same exchange hour’s Divine rates. Exchange prices are historical estimates. Gold fees are separate and are not deducted from profit.
