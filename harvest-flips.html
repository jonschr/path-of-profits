How estimates work
Expected output is the probability-weighted sale value of all eligible outcomes. Expected profit subtracts the input and lifeforce. Harvest rows model one roll, then selling the result; the held-item decision compares rerolling with selling now. Outcome weights carry their source dates and assumptions. A positive expectation can still lose money.
