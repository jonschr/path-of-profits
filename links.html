Tools worth using

PoE1 · Scarab vendoring

ScarabEV ↗

Observed 3-to-1 outcome weights, sell/reroll thresholds, stash search regex, inventory imports, and session logging.

Its session logger and inventory imports complement our exchange-based scarab calculator.

PoE1 · Harvest rerolls

Gains of Exile ↗

Compares selling now with continued Harvest rerolling, including lifeforce costs, stopping decisions, and price overrides.

Useful for repeated Harvest rolls and fossil yield models. Check probability dates; some weights come from older patches.

PoE2 · Reforging and crafting

PoE2 Calculator Suite ↗

Essence, catalyst, and soul-core reforgers; emotion upgrade stopping points; currency loops; corruption and Divine roll tools.

Useful specialized probability tools. Some require login or a paid account; their public feature descriptions were reviewed, not their gated calculations.

PoE1 · Recipe catalogue

PoeHow ↗

Vendor unique assembly, divination card rewards, tattoo/runegraft rerolls, and community drop-rate data.

A broad recipe and outcome catalogue is useful to retain as a reference alongside our execution-focused calculators.

PoE2 · Trading journal

Path of Trader ↗

Spread trading opportunities and a journal for realized results, committed capital, and trading performance.

Tracking executed trades answers a different question from estimated conversion margins; its existing journal is useful on its own.

PoE1 + PoE2 · Arbitrage

Divine Tendies ↗

Hourly cross-currency scans, trade playbooks, gold accounting, and PoE1 essence/oil vendor opportunities.

A useful second opinion for our existing arbitrage calculators, especially its full-cycle gold breakdown.
